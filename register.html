Rules and Regulations

1. Player Eligibility and Registration

  1. Participants must have been actively involved in Sharjah Jesus Youth for a minimum of one month in any of its prayer groups, ministries, or zonal units.
  2. The registration date will be considered the reference date for determining eligibility.
  3. Players are permitted to participate in both the Doubles and Mixed Doubles categories.
  4. Participation in the Mixed Doubles category is not limited to couples; any eligible male and female participants may form a team.
  5. Badminton shuttlecocks will be provided by the Organizing Committee.
  6. All matches shall be played using standard tournament-grade plastic shuttlecocks.
  7. Players must respect referees, opponents, fellow participants and tournament organizers at all times.
  8. Players are expected to maintain appropriate language, sportsmanship and a respectful code of conduct throughout the tournament.
  9. The referee's decisions regarding match conduct and scoring shall be final.
  10. All players must bring their own badminton rackets for the tournament.
  11. Participant eligibility shall be verified by the Organizing Committee in consultation with the relevant authorities.
  12. Any amendments or changes to the tournament rules will be communicated to all participants on or before 15 November.
  13. The registration fee for the doubles category is AED 30 per team.
  14. Registration will close on Thursday, 5th November. All participants are requested to complete their registration before the deadline.
  15. Eligible participants will be added to the temporary WhatsApp group. All participants must attend the meeting on Sunday, 8th November, for the tournament fixture selection. The time will be updated.
  16. On the same day, all tournament rules and regulations will be explained to the participants. They will also have the opportunity to ask questions and clarify any doubts before the fixture selection.
  17. All participants are required to pay the registration fee in cash when attending the fixture selection on Sunday, 8th November, to complete the registration process. Cash payment is mandatory, and bank transfers should be avoided.

2. Category A: Ages 12–18

  1. Eligible players must be between 12 and 18 years of age, subject to the tournament's specified cut-off date.
  2. Players must provide valid proof of age if requested by the organizers.
  3. Players may compete only in their registered age category.
  4. Matches in this category will be conducted in doubles only.
  5. Mixed doubles (male and female players as partners) will not be included in this category.

3. Category B: Ages 18 and Above

  1. Eligible players must be 18 years of age or older on the tournament date.
  2. All players must complete their registration before the registration deadline.

4. Match Rules

  1. All matches shall follow standard badminton rules.
  2. Matches shall be played as the best of three games.
  3. Each game shall be played to 21 points.
  4. If the score reaches 20–20, a player must win by a margin of two points. The game shall continue until a two-point lead is achieved, subject to a maximum score limit of 24 points.
  5. Players must arrive at the court at 5 PM.
  6. A player who fails to report within 10 minutes of the scheduled match time may be declared a walkover by the organizers.

5. Tournament Format

  1. Group Stage: Players shall be divided into groups according to the total number of registrations.
  2. Knockout Stage: The top-performing players from each group shall qualify for the quarter-finals or semi-finals, depending on the tournament draw and number of participants.
  3. Semi-Finals: Qualified players shall compete to determine the finalists.
  4. Final: The winners of the semi-final matches shall compete for the championship.
  5. The organizers shall announce the tournament draw and match schedule before the commencement of the tournament.
